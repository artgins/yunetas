Unreleased (APP_VERSION follows YUNETA_VERSION)
===============================================

-  Works again on the v7 SDK, with its original design: one input
   connection relayed both ways to one output connection.
-  The service gclass is C_SGATEWAY (main.c named 'Sgateway': gclass NOT
   FOUND, nothing ran).
-  The input side is a C_TCP_S (child_tree_filter), the output side a client
   C_TCP with `url`, started by the gateway (it is manual start). The urls
   are taken at each play.
-  The rates are updated (EV_TIMEOUT_PERIODIC), a closed side is seen as
   closed, and bytes for a side not open are dropped and counted (dropMsgs).
-  The yuno is played by the agent or by `yuno.autoplay`, not by itself.
-  A COPY of what one side sends goes to the other one, and the side read
   is paused until the other has written it (back-pressure). Up to 7.30.18
   the read buffer itself was handed over: corrupted data and one ERROR per
   message as soon as one side was slower.
-  One output connection per client: started when a client connects,
   stopped when it leaves; the client is dropped when its output closes.
-  `crypto` for a TLS output; the output's attempts back off up to 30 s.
-  No trace on by default; `Side open` / `Side closed` at the trace level
   `connections`.
-  A play while the output still waits for its write in flight no longer
   leaves the output dead: it starts at its EV_STOPPED.
-  A pause, or the stop of the yuno, with a client that does not read no
   longer starts the output again (7.30.19: a session with the backend
   while paused, and "Destroying a RUNNING gobj" at the stop).
-  `input_crypto` for a TLS input (tcps://): the `crypto` of its C_TCP_S.
   Up to 7.30.19 a TLS input could not be configured, and the yuno exited.
