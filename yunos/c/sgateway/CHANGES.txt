Unreleased (APP_VERSION follows YUNETA_VERSION)
===============================================

-  Works again on the v7 SDK, with its original design: one input
   connection relayed both ways to one output connection.
-  The service gclass is C_SGATEWAY (main.c named 'Sgateway': gclass NOT
   FOUND, nothing ran).
-  The input side is a C_TCP_S (child_tree_filter), the output side a client
   C_TCP with `url`, started by the gateway (it is manual start). The urls
   are taken at each play.
-  The rates are updated (EV_TIMEOUT_PERIODIC), a closed side is seen as
   closed, and bytes for a side not open are dropped and counted (dropMsgs).
-  The yuno is played by the agent or by `yuno.autoplay`, not by itself.
